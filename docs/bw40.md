# 40 MHz (HT40) on the top rungs — as built (2026-09-24)

Bench record: `docs/bw40-sweep-findings-2026-09-23.md` (the eight facts).
Design: spec 2026-09-24-bw40-design (gitignored). This page is what ships.

## What flies

- GS: `radio.width = 40`, every link card tuned HT40 on home's standard pair
  (home 136 → 132+136, primary = the upper half). A 20-tuned receiver cannot
  hear HT40; a 40-tuned one hears 20 MHz frames on the primary for ~0.2-1 dB.
- Drone: `radio.width = 40`, InitWrite on the same pair; airs EACH RUNG at the
  width the RCF profile byte names (`LayerTxSpec.bw`, no wire change).
- Ladder (`gs/bundle/maburgs.default.toml`): 20/0, 20/1, 20/2, 20/3, 20/4,
  40/3, 40/4. HT40 MCS3 (54 Mb/s) reaches 1-1.6 dB further than HT20 MCS5 at
  more capacity; HT40 MCS4 (81 Mb/s) reaches like HT20 MCS6. Bottom rungs stay
  20 (HT40 MCS0 reaches 4-6 dB less than HT20 MCS0). Reshape = config edit.
- Bitrate cap unchanged (24 Mb/s): the 40 rungs buy airtime, not bitrate.
- Control and telemetry frames are pinned to MCS0 / 20 MHz / LDPC+STBC with
  `TxMode::no_agg` (`drone/src/control_tx_mode.h`). The probe canary is
  different: it flies the PROBE RUNG's own `(mcs, bw)` — `to_tx_mode(*probe,
  probe->bw)` — and shares only `no_agg` with control/telemetry
  (`drone/src/radio_tx.cpp`'s probe layer). Sitting on rung 20/4, the probe
  therefore airs 40/3, not 20/anything — that is the point of the GS
  probe-profile change: `no_agg` keeps every one of these frames a PPDU of
  its own so an A-MPDU session never folds them into a video aggregate at
  the aggregate's rate/width, and the probe still measures the NEXT rung's
  own width. Video layers aggregate per `ampdu.min_mcs_20/_40`.

## Per-width tables (drone)

`air_clock.efficiency_20/_40` (delivered/nominal per MCS; bundle: 20 MHz
`[0.93, 0.88, 0.84, 0.80, 0.76, 0.76, 0.78, 0.76]`, 40 MHz a flat
`[0.77, 0.75, 0.75, 0.75, 0.74, 0.77, 0.76, 0.75]` with agg6) and
`ampdu.min_mcs_20 = 4` / `min_mcs_40 = 2`. `delivered_mbps` and
`ampdu_mode_for` pick by the rung's `bw`. All four keys are optional in the
drone config (the spec said required): an absent `efficiency_20/_40`
defaults to nominal (1.0 per MCS) and an absent `min_mcs_20/_40` to 0. The
bundle sets all four and `tests/test_config.cpp` pins them.

## Channels: one offset for the whole plan

FastRetune keeps width AND offset on both ends. With home 136 (offset 2,
primary = upper half) a retune to 140 would land on the off-grid 136+140.
Rule (enforced by GS config): `radio.scan.candidates` are pair PRIMARIES with
the same `ht40_offset` as home. Shipped: `[144, 40, 128]` = 140+144 (the only
fast retune from 132+136, 2-12 ms), 36+40 (UNII-1, spur-free), 124+128 (DFS,
spur-free). 116+120, 149+153, 157+161 are 8822E spur centres (65-91 ms
retunes); 165 has no pair.

## Boot scan scores every half; the pick is a pair

The boot scout card InitWrites at 20 MHz and dwells on both halves of home's
pair and of every candidate's pair (`pair_pick.h` `scan_half_set`: 132, 136,
140, 144, 36, 40, 124, 128). The ranker scores halves as before; a pair is
ranked once BOTH halves reach `min_rounds`, scores as its WORSE half, home's
pair keeps `home_margin`, ties go home (`pair_proposal`). The proposal is the
pair's primary, so DISC, ChannelPlan and the drone see one channel number. At
`radio.width = 40` the boot pick counts as "measured" — fed to
`HopRanker::set_boot_pick` and logged non-`none` in `scan.log`'s `K` line —
only when some pair is ranked under that same both-halves rule
(`pair_pick.h`'s `any_pair_ranked`); a lone ranked half (e.g. the one-card
home half, which also books home-window visits) is not a pick. At the freeze
the card does ONE full `SetMonitorChannel` to 40 on the op pair
(`RadioFrontend::set_width`, `"maburgs radio: card N width 20 -> 40 MHz on ch
<ch>"` on stderr) and joins RX diversity. One-card mode: 20 until the freeze,
40 after. Width is desired per-card state (`RadioFrontend::width()`; a
`set_width` on a down card is recorded and the revive InitWrites at it), and
two paths skip the freeze-time switch — the scout card dying mid-scan, and
the first DISC_ACK freezing the plan before the scout thread ever started —
so the core loop also resyncs: once no scout thread owns a card and the scan
is frozen, any ready card not at `radio.width` gets one `set_width` on its
live channel per bring-up (`gs/src/width_resync.h`).

## In-flight scout: primary-only, by design

Dwells keep the card's 40 MHz tuning (FastRetune), so they score the
candidate's primary only — a 40 MHz dwell is nearly blind to the secondary
(findings "Scouting"). The in-flight `HopRanker` is built over the same
primaries as at 20 MHz — home plus `radio.scan.candidates`. Nothing from the
boot scan reaches it (only the in-flight dwells and freshness bursts, both on
primaries), and every candidate is a pair primary sharing home's offset, so
every ranked channel is a valid hop target: FastRetune keeps the card's
offset, and a hop to a secondary half (136 → 140 tunes the off-grid
136+140) is simply not in the set. A busy secondary on the op pair shows up as probe failures on the
two 40 rungs; the ladder holds the 20 rungs, which sit on the primary. Cost:
the best 20 rung is MCS4 (~19 Mb/s at the 0.65 budget) vs the 24 Mb/s cap the
40 rungs reach. Per-half in-flight dwells (a width switch per dwell) are a
later spec.

## Observability

- ctl.log `ctllog 12`: header `ladder=bw:mcs/ovb:ove,...`; no column change.
- fec.log `feclog 2` and probe.log `probelog 3`: a `bw` column after `mcs`,
  so 20/3 and 40/3 do not pool; flightreport groups by (mcs, bw).
- flightreport `BW40` section: time held on 40 MHz rungs, promotes onto them
  vs held past 3 s; "every promote fell straight back" = suspect the secondary.
- scan.log `scanlog 3`: D line ends with the dwell's width; K line ends with
  `pair=<lo>+<hi>`.
- Sideport (v 1): `link.ctl.rung.bw`, `link.ctl.ladder[].bw`, `link.rungs[].bw`,
  `link.streams[].rung_bw`; maburtop `mcs3/40`, compact OSD `mcs:3/40`.
- Drone stderr: `maburd radio: A-MPDU ON at mcs3/40 (min_mcs_40 2, max_num 6)`.

## Deploy (no flag day)

Config before binary on each end. Drone first (a 40-tuned drone still hears
the 20 MHz uplink; a 20-tuned GS never commands a 40 rung unless its config
has one), then GS. Keep `*.pre-bw40` binaries and configs on both devices.
Static pin: `link.static_mcs` + `link.static_bw` (40 needs `radio.width 40`).

## Not measured / later

Per-half in-flight dwells; HT40 MCS5; raising the cap; the 149/153 deficit; maburcal
at 40 MHz; the 20-tuned receiver's weak-signal loss (sidestepped, not fixed);
secondary desense of the 20 MHz rungs (watch the BW40 section);
`tools/bench/switchloss.py` parses the ctllog 12 `bw:` prefix but still keys
rungs by MCS only, so its rate math is blind to a bw-only rung change.

and configs kept on both devices; drone config = the device's tuned file with
the three renamed/added keys, GS config = `gs/bundle/maburgs.default.toml`

`hear` = `drone.rcf.rx_pps` / GS card `tx_pps`. Drone `low_power` was
